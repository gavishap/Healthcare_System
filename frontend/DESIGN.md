# Evidence Desk design direction

An analyst workbench for US health system strategy research. Calm paper surfaces, a compact left rail, readable evidence records, restrained forest/teal accents and amber coverage flags. The working hierarchy is scope, source coverage, briefing, citation register, conflicts and explicit policy.

Applied the vault's UI/UX Pro Max skill: inspected a design-system search and retried with `internal analytics data dashboard`. The data-dense-dashboard style, restrained motion, evidence tables, keyboard controls, readable contrast and explicit source states fit the application. Its generated enterprise marketing pattern and initial wellness/neumorphism suggestion do not fit and are not used. No verified marketing pattern was adopted. This document is the deliberate application-specific fallback, not a claim to implement an official design system.

Use native semantic controls, a focus-managed native dialog, visible focus states, color plus text status, responsive record rows, reduced motion and immutable source details. No invented charts, sample metrics or fabricated testimonials. All live content comes from the API; synthetic fixtures are explicitly labeled.
