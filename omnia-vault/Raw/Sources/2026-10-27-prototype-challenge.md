## Page 1

AI Prototype Challenge 
Forward-deployed AI consultants solve real business problems with AI—fast. You're embedded 
with clients, turning ambiguous challenges into working solutions that ship. 
A prospective client has approached your firm with a business challenge. Your job is to: 
• Build a working prototype that demonstrates a viable AI solution 
• Prepare a single slide that pitches your solution to client stakeholders 
• Demo and defend your approach in a live interview 
We're evaluating solution design, technical execution, communication, and how you work under 
constraints. Time is tight by design, and tradeoffs are expected—be efficient, be pragmatic, and 
get creative. We'd rather see clever problem-solving than pixel-perfect polish. 
Scenarios 
Choose one scenario that best showcases your strengths: 
• Manufacturing: A plant operator wants floor supervisors to get answers from the right 
documentation. The system should intelligently route questions to the appropriate source 
(safety procedures, maintenance manuals, or quality control standards) and provide accurate 
answers. 
• Finance: A wealth management firm wants a solution that helps advisors quickly get up to 
speed on a stock—fetching current quote data and summarizing key information from recent 
SEC filings. 
• Healthcare: A health system strategy team wants a tool that generates a structured briefing for 
a given medical condition, covering the current standard of care, emerging treatments in 
development, and key companies/institutions involved. 
• Open Choice: Design an AI solution that addresses a challenge in healthcare, finance, or 
manufacturing. You define the problem, the user, and the solution. 
Environment & Data 
You have flexibility in how you build your solution. Choose the approach that best demonstrates 
your capabilities.  
Examples of approaches: 
• No-Code / Low-Code: n8n 
• AI-Native Tools: Custom GPTs, Gemini, Claude Projects, NotebookLM, Perplexity 
• Code + Frameworks: VS Code, Python, LangChain, FastAPI, Streamlit 
• Hybrid: Any combination of the above 
An n8n workspace and Portkey API access will be provided. For AI-native tools (Custom GPTs, 
Gemini, Claude Projects, NotebookLM, Perplexity, etc.), you're welcome to use free tiers or your 
own accounts—we won't provide platform access for these. Data is not provided—you are 
responsible for sourcing or creating your own.

Deliverables 
1. Working Prototype (120 minutes recommended) 
• Must be demonstrable via live demo 
• Does not need to be production-ready 
• Interface approach is up to you—chat, form, report, API, or something else 
• Focus on core functionality over polish 
2. One Slide (10 minutes recommended) 
• Single slide to support your pitch 
• Treat the interview panel as client stakeholders 
• Structure and content are yours to decide 
3. Live Interview (20 minutes) 
• Present your slide and demonstrate your prototype 
• We'll ask questions as client stakeholders evaluating your solution
